## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered specifically for premium menswear boutiques, bespoke tailoring houses, and luxury fabric merchants. The aesthetic combines modern executive precision with tactile retail efficiency, balancing the prestige of an upscale sartorial atelier with the lightning-fast, high-stress demands of a busy Point of Sale terminal.

The emotional core of the interface is unyielding clarity, premium calm, and authoritative reliability. Store consultants, tailors, and checkout managers handle complex multi-attribute transactions (custom suiting yardage, finishing additions, alterations, and bulk luxury fabric roll cuts). The UI eliminates operational friction and cognitive overload through crisp micro-contrast, decisive visual hierarchies, and generous, error-free physical touch affordances.

The design movement mixes **Modern Executive Minimalism** with subtle **Tactile Precision**:
- High-contrast typography instantly communicates fabric codes, bolt stock quantities, cut lengths, and transaction totals in Pakistani Rupee (PKR).
- Layout density respects both standing cashier interactions and close-up tabletop consultations on 10-inch to 15-inch retail touch screens.
- Physical boundaries, tactile states, and decisive color-coded feedback guarantee rapid checkouts with zero ambiguity.

## Colors

The palette establishes an executive, pristine environment optimized for high-brightness boutique settings and fast, mistake-free operation:

- **Canvas & Backgrounds:** Crisp slate-tinted white foundations (`#F8FAFC` for root canvases, `#F1F5F9` for secondary structural sidebars and order summaries).
- **Surfaces & Cards:** Pure `#FFFFFF` for catalog tiles, customer alteration dockets, and interactive inputs, bound by structural borders in `#E2E8F0`.
- **Primary Brand / Headers:** Deep Midnight Navy (`#0F172A` deep base, `#1E293B` hover/secondary elements). It provides relentless contrast for text, titles, selected tabs, and primary system controls.
- **Accent & Checkout Completion:** Rich Emerald Green (`#10B981` primary, `#059669` hover/pressed). Reserved specifically for the critical path: final payment confirmations, successful balance settlements, invoice validation, and active balance clear states.
- **Danger & Reversal:** Vivid Crimson (`#EF4444` base, `#DC2626` hover/pressed). Used exclusively for irreversible actions: voiding line items, aborting transactions, fabric roll waste deduction, and return adjustments.
- **Text & Hierarchy:** Deep charcoal (`#0F172A`) for all primary metrics, customer names, and PKR price calculations. Muted slate (`#64748B`) for fabric specs, GSM weights, unit meters, and metadata.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** for its exceptional optical clarity, geometric structure, and generous x-height, ensuring legibility at an arm’s length across touch screens and merchant displays.

Key typographic rules:
- **Price Format:** All prices are displayed with explicit currency notation (e.g., `PKR 48,500`). The currency prefix uses medium weight (`label-md` or `label-sm`), while the numerical amount is set in high-emphasis semibold or bold (`price-hero`, `price-tag`) to prevent misreading transaction figures.
- **Numbers & Units:** Tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be used for all receipt lines, yardage counts (e.g., `4.5 m`, `3.25 yds`), phone numbers, and subtotal columns to maintain strict vertical alignment.
- **All Caps:** Restrict uppercase usage exclusively to SKU identifiers, fabric composition tags (e.g., `100% GIZA COTTON`, `SUPER 150S WOOL`), and receipt status pills.

## Layout & Spacing

The terminal layout is structured as a dedicated **fixed-column split workspace** optimized for rapid dual-handed POS workflows:

- **Desktop & Countertop Terminals (>=1024px):**
  - Left pane (Catalog & Fabric Matrix): Dynamic 8-column layout with 16px gutters (`gutter`) displaying fabric categories, cloth swatches, ready-to-wear items, and tailoring packages.
  - Right pane (Cart & Checkout Ledger): Fixed 420px width on standard 1080p terminals (or 4 columns in a 12-column grid with `gutter-lg` 24px) that holds the active bill, cut quantities, tax breakdown, customer tailoring profile, and primary payment trigger.
- **Tablet / Mobile Floor Devices (<1024px):**
  - Stacks into a full-width browsing experience with a persistent bottom order sheet (`margin: 1rem`) displaying active item count, running subtotal in PKR, and an expansive "Review & Tender" touch button.
- **Touch-Target Rhythm:**
  - Every interactive component adheres to a strict physical baseline: **minimum 48px height and width** (extending to 56px–64px for core numeric keypad entries and the checkout tender CTA).
  - Internal card padding uses `space-md` (16px) to keep tiles compact yet fully scannable.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids murky or heavy skeuomorphic shadows, employing a crisp architectural hierarchy composed of **subtle structural borders** and **focused ambient lifts**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas):** Tone `#F8FAFC` and `#F1F5F9`. Flat, non-reflective base.
- **Surface Level (Tiles, Inputs, Bills):** Tone `#FFFFFF` with a 1px boundary (`#E2E8F0`). No shadow in passive state to ensure edge-to-edge optical purity.
- **Interactive Hover & Drag Lift:** When a catalog item, fabric roll tile, or custom measurement card is pressed or selected, it raises with an ambient micro-lift: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`, paired with an active primary ring border in `#0F172A`.
- **Floating Modals & Tender Drawers:** Elevated payment drawers, customer search overlays, and discount numpads use a crisp boundary backed by `0 20px 30px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.
- **Divider Treatment:** Inner dividers in order ledgers use a hairline 1px `#E2E8F0` solid rule, preserving structured scanning across long fabric dockets.

## Shapes

The design uses a refined curvature profile (`roundedness: 2` base) calibrated to convey executive elegance while retaining the punchy structure required of retail touch hardware:

- **Standard Controls (`rounded`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form fields, quantity selectors, and table rows.
- **Catalog Cards & Tiles (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Fabric bolt cards, tailoring suit silhouettes, and order list containers.
- **Hero Checkout Actions & Keypad Nodes (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Payment buttons, currency quick-cash denomination pills, and customer loyalty badges.
- **Badges & Micro Chips:** Set with fully rounded pill radii (`9999px`) for clear categorical differentiation.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Touch Triggers
- **Primary Tender Button:** Minimum height 56px (up to 64px on main payment actions). Background `#10B981`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight bold (`label-lg`), radius `rounded-xl`. Active tap feedback shifts to `#059669` with a brief scale reduction (`scale-98`). Prominently features the final amount in PKR.
- **Secondary / Action Buttons:** Minimum height 48px. Background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `rounded-lg`. Hover/active shifts to `#1E293B`.
- **Outline / Utility Buttons:** Minimum height 48px. Pure `#FFFFFF` background, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`.
- **Destructive Button:** Minimum height 48px. Soft crimson tint background (`#FEF2F2`), border `1px solid #FECACA`, text `#EF4444`. Direct clear/void actions use solid `#EF4444` with `#FFFFFF` text.

### 2. Catalog & Fabric Cards
- Dimensions: Equal aspect ratio grid tiles or 80px horizontal row cards.
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, radius `rounded-lg` (16px).
- Displays fabric mill name, blend (e.g., Loro Piana 130s, Egyptian Cotton), available meterage bar, and bold PKR price per meter (`price-tag`).
- Out-of-stock items feature an overlay with high-contrast badge and disabled tap states.

### 3. Price Display & Receipt Ledger
- Ledger rows have 52px minimum height, separated by 1px `#E2E8F0` borders.
- Item titles in `#0F172A` with yardage/meterage calculation in `#64748B` (e.g., `4.00 m × PKR 6,500/m`).
- Subtotal, Tax (Sales Tax / SRB / PRA), and Grand Total grouped in a `#F1F5F9` elevated summary box. Grand total rendered in `price-hero` font (`PKR XX,XXX`).

### 4. Input Fields & Numeric Keypad
- Inputs have minimum 48px height, 16px horizontal padding, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, and `rounded-lg` corners.
- On focus: 2px sharp outline ring in `#0F172A`.
- Touch Numeric Keypad: Large 64px circular or rounded-lg rectangular buttons with tabular numbers in `headline-md` weight.

### 5. Chips, Tags & Selection Pills
- Height: 38px to 44px for effortless fingertip selection.
- Fabric category chips (Boski, Khaddar, Wool, Silk, Linen) sit on an edge-scrolling top rail.
- Inactive state: `#F1F5F9` surface with `#0F172A` label.
- Selected state: Deep Navy `#0F172A` background with crisp white `#FFFFFF` text.

### 6. Tailoring & Alteration Specifics (Bespoke Add-on)
- Measurement drawer cards: Split fields for collar, cuff, chest, waist, and length measurements with oversized stepper controls (+ / - buttons at 48px each).
- Fitting Status Indicators: Status tags rendered in Emerald Green (Ready), Midnight Blue (In Cutting), and Amber/Slate (Under Trial).
